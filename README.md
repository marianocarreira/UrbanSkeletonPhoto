# Urban Skeleton

A curated photography repository capturing the intersection of artistic expression and city infrastructure. 

## Vision & Philosophy

This project explores the city not just as a background, but as a living canvas. The photographs documented here analyze how art interacts with, challenges, and enhances the physical and social landscape of the urban environment through five distinct lenses:

### 🏢 Architectural Integration
We focus on how street art interacts with building designs, structural materials, and raw surfaces. It is an exploration of how paint, textures, and interventions adapt to concrete, glass, or brick, turning rigid walls into cultural dialogue.

### 📐 Urban Planning & Scale
The images examine the relationship between art and the layout of the city. From massive murals visible from highways to hidden interventions in narrow alleys, this perspective observes how art occupies public space, alters human flow, and scales itself to the surrounding infrastructure.

### 🧱 Structural Foundation
Highlighting the raw skeleton of the city—bridges, pillars, industrial zones, and brutalist elements. These photographs look for geometry, lines, shadows, and angles where heavy engineering meets spontaneous or curated artistic expressions.

### 👥 The Social Fabric
Urban art is an inherent reflection of social structures, community identity, and political movements. This repository documents the invisible layers of the city: the voices, struggles, and histories left behind on the walls by the people who inhabit these spaces.

### ⚡ Spontaneous Composition
Street photography is bound to the unpredictable. We capture the ephemeral harmony of a passing shadow, a random pedestrian walking past a mural, or the natural decay of a wall—moments where timing and the chaos of the city accidentally create the perfect frame.

---
*All photographs in this repository are original works. Feel free to explore, analyze, and appreciate the hidden geometry of our streets.*
